Privacy Policy – PDF Convert Free

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Summary

PDF Convert Free (app and website) is made by Dr. Kaushikkumar Vadadoriya, PEDIA360, Surat, Gujarat, India. Your files are processed entirely on your own device. We do not upload, store, read or share your documents, photos or scans.

Files and documents

  • All conversions, scans, edits, compression and text recognition (OCR) run inside the app on your phone or computer.
  • Your files are never sent to our servers or to anyone else. We do not have servers that receive files.
  • Files you create can be kept in the app's Files tab. They are stored only on your device, and you can delete them at any time (Settings → Clear saved files, or by clearing the app's data).

Camera

The camera is used only when you choose to scan a document. The camera picture is processed on your device and is not recorded or sent anywhere. You can deny or remove camera permission in your phone's settings; you can still pick photos from your gallery.

Internet use

The app downloads its own program files and helper libraries (for example the PDF, Word and text-recognition engines and language data) from public content delivery networks (cdnjs.cloudflare.com, cdn.jsdelivr.net) and fonts from Google Fonts. These requests contain no file content. Like any website request, these providers may see your device's IP address and browser type.

Personal data, ads and tracking

  • We do not ask for an account, name, email or phone number.
  • We do not show ads and do not use analytics or tracking cookies.
  • Your settings (language, scanner options) are saved only on your device.

Children

The app is a general-purpose document tool and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
